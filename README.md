# 🎓 Alumni Portal

A centralized web-based platform designed to connect **Students**, **Alumni**, **Industry Partners**, and **Administrators** in a single digital ecosystem. The platform strengthens relationships between the university and its stakeholders by enabling professional networking, mentorship, career development, and industry collaboration.

---

## 📖 Overview

The Alumni Portal serves as a bridge between the university and its alumni while providing opportunities for students to connect with experienced professionals and industry partners. It streamlines communication, job recruitment, event management, and mentorship through a modern, secure, and scalable web application.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student
- Connect with alumni for mentorship and career guidance.
- Explore internship and job opportunities.
- Build and manage professional profiles.
- Stay updated with university events and announcements.

### 🎓 Alumni
- Create and manage professional profiles.
- Upload and update CVs/resumes.
- Search and apply for jobs.
- Participate in mentorship programs.
- Register for university events.
- Receive university news and announcements.

### 🏢 Industry
- Post job and internship opportunities.
- Recruit talented students and alumni.
- Participate in university recruitment initiatives.
- Engage with the university community.

### 🛠️ Administrator
- Manage users and role assignments.
- Verify accounts.
- Publish announcements.
- Organize and manage events.
- Monitor overall platform activities.

## 🏗️ Architecture

The project follows a **Modular Monolith Architecture**, providing:

- Clean separation of modules
- High maintainability
- Scalability
- Simplified deployment
- Easier feature management

- Admin

## 🔐 Security

The platform implements **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** to ensure that users can only access resources and features permitted by their assigned roles.

Authentication and authorization mechanisms help:
- Protect sensitive information
- Restrict unauthorized access
- Deliver a personalized experience for each user
